# Greeting page

Read `greeting` and `appName` from `.blueprint/answers.json`. Add a page showing them — `src/pages/Hello.vue` in a
Vue app, or `public/hello.html` if the app has no pages yet. Nothing else.

Done when the check passes: one of those two files exists and is not empty.
